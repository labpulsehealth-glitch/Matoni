Women's Health Conditions

Some health conditions affect reproductive organs, hormones and menstrual patterns. Others can affect several systems throughout the body.

Understanding these conditions can help women ask informed questions and seek appropriate medical care.

01. Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS)

PCOS is a hormonal and metabolic condition that may affect ovulation, menstrual regularity and other aspects of health.

Possible features include:

  • Irregular or absent periods.
  • Increased facial or body hair.
  • Acne or oily skin.
  • Difficulty with ovulation.

Diagnosis requires clinical assessment. One symptom alone does not confirm PCOS.

02. Endometriosis

Endometriosis is a condition in which tissue similar to the lining of the uterus grows outside the uterus.

Possible features include:

  • Painful periods.
  • Persistent pelvic pain.
  • Pain during sexual activity.
  • Pain with bowel movements or urination, especially around periods.
  • Difficulty becoming pregnant for some people.

Symptoms vary, and some people may have few or no obvious symptoms.

03. Uterine Fibroids

Fibroids are growths that develop in or around the uterus. They are usually non-cancerous.

Possible features include:

  • Heavy or prolonged menstrual bleeding.
  • Pelvic pressure or discomfort.
  • Frequent urination.
  • Abdominal enlargement in some cases.

Some people with fibroids have no symptoms and may not require treatment.

04. Adenomyosis

Adenomyosis occurs when tissue similar to the uterine lining grows into the muscular wall of the uterus.

Possible features include:

  • Heavy menstrual bleeding.
  • Painful periods.
  • Pelvic discomfort.
  • A feeling of pressure or fullness in the pelvis.

A healthcare professional can assess symptoms and determine what investigations may be appropriate.

Why Awareness Matters

Pain, heavy bleeding and persistent changes in menstrual patterns should not automatically be dismissed.

Early conversations with healthcare professionals can help people understand their symptoms and available options.
