A MATONI INITIATIVE

PERIOD WITHOUT SHAME.

Menstruation is biology. Dignity is a right. No girl should ever feel ashamed of her period.

Why This Initiative Exists

Across many communities, menstruation is surrounded by silence, embarrassment, misinformation and stigma.

Girls may be mocked for menstrual stains, excluded from activities, prevented from discussing their periods or forced to manage menstruation without adequate products.

Period Without Shame exists to challenge these experiences through education, dignity, access and community engagement.

01. Menstrual Education

Helping girls understand menstruation, the menstrual cycle, menstrual hygiene, period products and normal bodily changes.

02. Menstrual Dignity

Challenging the belief that menstruation is dirty, embarrassing or something girls should hide.

A menstrual stain is not a reason to laugh at, humiliate or disrespect someone.

03. Menstrual Product Access

Working towards better access to menstrual products for girls who cannot easily afford them.

MATONI envisions partnerships with schools, NGOs, pharmacies, manufacturers, community organisations and donors.

Explore menstrual support

04. Supportive Environments

Encouraging schools, families and communities to create environments where girls can manage menstruation safely and without humiliation.

Our School Outreach Programme

Period Without Shame will extend beyond the digital platform through school-based awareness and educational activities.

Girls will receive menstrual-health education and practical guidance. Boys will receive age-appropriate education about menstruation, privacy and respect. Teachers and parents will be encouraged to support menstrual dignity.

The purpose is to build an environment where girls can learn, participate and grow without menstrual stigma.

What We Hope to Change

  • Improve menstrual-health knowledge.
  • Reduce menstrual stigma and bullying.
  • Improve awareness of menstrual product options.
  • Encourage supportive school environments.
  • Help girls feel confident discussing their health.

Menstruation Is Not Shameful.

Every girl deserves knowledge, dignity and access to the resources she needs.
