PERIOD WITHOUT SHAME

Menstruation is a normal biological process. It is not dirty, disgusting, embarrassing or something that makes anyone less worthy of respect.

We are working towards a world where every girl can understand her period, access the products she needs and attend school without fear of humiliation.

Find menstrual support
OUR BELIEF

Menstruation is not a reason for shame.

A girl should not have to hide a sanitary pad, fear a stain on her uniform, miss school because she is menstruating or tolerate jokes about her body.

Boys should understand menstruation too. We do not want boys to feel sorry for girls. We want them to understand the biology and treat menstruating people with respect.

OUR FOUR AREAS OF ACTION

Changing knowledge, attitudes and access.

01. Education

Age-appropriate menstrual health education for girls and boys, including puberty, menstrual biology and menstrual hygiene.

02. Respect

Prevent period-related teasing, humiliation, bullying, rumours and discrimination in schools and communities.

03. Access

Connect girls who cannot afford menstrual products with potential support from verified partners, schools and community organisations.

04. School participation

Help schools create environments where students can manage menstruation privately, safely and confidently.

THE PERIOD RESPECT CLASSROOM

Schools should be safe places to learn.

We envision a school programme that equips schools with educational materials, teacher guidance, menstrual product support pathways and a framework for preventing menstrual bullying.

Participating schools could receive:

  • Age-appropriate classroom materials.
  • Separate and joint learning sessions where appropriate.
  • Teacher and school staff training.
  • Menstrual health clubs and peer education resources.
  • Guidance for responding to period-related bullying.
  • Information about menstrual product access.
WHO WE EDUCATE

Everyone has a role to play.

Girls

Understand your body, prepare for your first period, manage menstruation and know that you deserve respect.

Boys

Understand menstrual biology, reject harmful stereotypes and learn how to treat classmates with dignity.

Enter boys' learning

Teachers

Learn how to respond appropriately when a student starts menstruating, needs assistance or experiences period-related bullying.

Parents and guardians

Build confidence discussing menstruation and supporting children through puberty.

MEASURING OUR IMPACT

We want to change real experiences.

In future school pilots, we aim to evaluate changes in menstrual health knowledge, attitudes, bullying experiences, access to menstrual products and period-related school participation.

Any evaluation involving children will require appropriate safeguarding, consent and ethical oversight.
