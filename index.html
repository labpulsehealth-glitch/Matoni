WHY WE EXIST

Women's health deserves more understanding.

Too many women experience unanswered health questions, dismissed symptoms, barriers to healthcare and gaps in scientific knowledge. Girls also grow up in environments where natural changes in their bodies are treated with shame.

HER KNOWS brings research, education, technology and community engagement together to help change that.

OUR FOUNDING PRINCIPLES
