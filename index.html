Welcome to MATONI

MATONI is a girls- and women-centred health technology initiative working to improve women's health knowledge, access to reliable information, menstrual dignity and understanding of the female body.

We believe that no girl should grow up ashamed of her body, confused about her health or unable to access trustworthy information.

Our Beliefs

  • No girl should be ashamed of her body.
  • Women's symptoms should never be casually dismissed.
  • Every girl deserves reliable health education.
  • Women's health research deserves greater attention.
  • Personal health information deserves privacy and protection.
  • Menstrual dignity should not depend on family income.
