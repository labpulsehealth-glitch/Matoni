THE SCIENCE OF WOMEN'S HEALTH

Better research. Better understanding. Better healthcare.

We want to help close gaps in women's health knowledge through scientific collaboration, responsible technology and accessible research communication.

HERRESEARCH

What do we still need to understand about women's bodies?

Women's health deserves rigorous scientific investigation. We aim to support research that examines female physiology, health conditions, treatment experiences and differences across life stages.

Our research priorities

Female hormones

Hormonal changes and their relationships with health throughout life.

Medication and treatment experiences

How treatments work, their effects and potential differences in women's experiences.

Female-specific conditions

Research into PCOS, endometriosis, fibroids and other conditions affecting women.

Menstrual and reproductive health

Understanding menstrual disorders, reproductive health needs and access to care.

Women's health across the lifespan

Health needs during adolescence, adulthood, pregnancy and menopause.

Research representation

Examining how women are represented in biomedical research and whether relevant findings are analysed appropriately.

WOMEN IN SCIENCE

Making research more inclusive.

We envision partnerships with universities, research institutions, healthcare professionals and scientists to identify evidence gaps and improve public understanding of women's health research.

RESEARCH ETHICS

Trust must come before data.

Participation in research must be voluntary and based on informed consent. Studies involving people must receive appropriate ethical review and follow applicable privacy and safeguarding requirements.

We will not sell women's personal health information. Any future research data resource must have strict governance, security and access controls.

Our long-term ambition

HERRESEARCH is envisioned as a research division within HER KNOWS, with the long-term ambition of developing into an independent research institute and collaborating with institutions internationally.

This research programme is currently a vision under development. We are not yet conducting clinical studies or collecting participant health data.
