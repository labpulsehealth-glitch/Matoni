HERRESEARCH

Better research. Better understanding. Better futures for women and girls.

Women's health deserves serious scientific attention. Their experiences deserve to be studied, their questions deserve investigation, and their contributions deserve recognition.

Explore our research vision
WHY HERRESEARCH EXISTS

Women's health should never be an afterthought.

For generations, important questions about women's bodies, health conditions, hormones, medications and experiences have needed greater scientific attention.

HERRESEARCH is MATONI's proposed research and knowledge division. Its long-term vision is to encourage scientific discovery, support collaboration, improve representation and make research opportunities more accessible.

We want to create an environment where girls can become researchers, students can find mentors, scientists can collaborate, and important questions about women's health receive the attention they deserve.

WHAT DESERVES MORE ATTENTION

Research without blind spots.

These are areas we believe deserve sustained research, scientific discussion and investment.

01

Hormones and the female body

Menstrual cycles, hormonal changes, puberty, fertility, endocrine health, menopause and the ways hormones influence the body throughout life.

02

Medication and treatment

How medicines affect women, differences in treatment responses, medication safety, side effects and the representation of women in clinical research.

03

Female-specific conditions

Conditions such as endometriosis, polycystic ovary syndrome, fibroids, adenomyosis and other conditions that require better understanding and diagnosis.

04

Menstrual and reproductive health

Menstrual disorders, reproductive health, fertility, pregnancy, postpartum experiences and access to reliable healthcare.

05

Mental and emotional wellbeing

The relationship between physical health, hormonal changes, psychological wellbeing, social pressures and women's lived experiences.

06

Maternal health and childbirth

Pregnancy complications, maternal mortality, childbirth experiences, postpartum recovery and the quality of maternal healthcare.

07

Ageing and menopause

Women's health across adulthood and later life, including menopause, bone health, cardiovascular health and age-related changes.

08

Health inequality and access

Differences in healthcare access, diagnosis, affordability, research representation and treatment outcomes across communities.

09

Environment and women's health

How pollution, occupational exposures, environmental conditions, nutrition and lifestyle factors may affect women's health.

10

Technology and innovation

Femtech, diagnostic technologies, health data, artificial intelligence and innovations that can improve women's healthcare.

OUR NON-NEGOTIABLE PRINCIPLES

Research must respect the people it serves.

Scientific progress must never come at the expense of human dignity, safety or fundamental rights.

Ethical approval

Research involving human participants must receive the appropriate ethics review and regulatory approvals.

Informed consent

Participants must receive understandable information and freely agree to participate under appropriate safeguards.

Privacy and confidentiality

Personal information and sensitive health data must be protected and handled responsibly.

Fair representation

Research should recognise differences in populations and avoid unfair exclusion, discrimination and harmful bias.

Research integrity

Scientific work must be honest, transparent, properly attributed and free from fabrication or manipulation.

Protection from exploitation

Participants, students and researchers deserve protection from coercion, harassment, exploitation and abuse of power.

THE LONG-TERM VISION

From a platform to a research institution.

HERRESEARCH is envisioned as more than a page containing information and opportunities.

Our long-term ambition is to build the foundation for a research ecosystem that supports scientific collaboration, research funding, mentorship, knowledge exchange and meaningful discoveries in women's health.

In the future, this vision may grow into a dedicated research institute supporting interdisciplinary research and partnerships across Africa and beyond.

This is an ambition for the future, not a claim that such an institute or research programme is already operational.

Where we are today

MATONI is developing its research vision and prototype. The collaboration network, funding hub, opportunities directory and research institute described on this page are planned developments.

MATONI is not currently conducting clinical research through HERRESEARCH or collecting research participant health data through this page.
