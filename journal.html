Your Privacy Matters

This prototype saves entries in the local storage of your current browser and device. They are not uploaded to MATONI or shared with another person.

This is not encrypted medical-record storage. Someone with access to your unlocked device or browser may be able to view these entries. Avoid recording information you would not want another device user to see, and delete your entries before handing over a shared device.

Clearing browser data may also erase your journal.
