Cycle Estimate

Enter the first day of your most recent period and your usual cycle length to see an approximate next-period date.

Understanding Cycle Length

Cycle length is counted from the first day of one period to the day before the next period begins.

Adult menstrual cycles commonly range from 21 to 35 days. Cycles can be more variable during adolescence.

Keep in Mind

This is an estimate, not a prediction. Stress, illness, hormonal changes and other factors can affect cycle timing. A calendar calculation cannot confirm ovulation or pregnancy.

Your Privacy Comes First

This prototype performs its calculation in your browser. It does not save your dates, create an account or send your information to MATONI.

If you are using a shared device, avoid entering personal health information.
