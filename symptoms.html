Your symptoms deserve attention.

Understand common menstrual symptoms, recognise changes and learn when to seek professional care.

Understanding Your Body

People experience menstruation differently. Some have mild symptoms, while others experience symptoms that affect their daily activities.

A symptom being common does not mean you must silently endure it.

Menstrual Cramps

Cramping can occur when the uterus contracts to shed its lining. Some people experience mild discomfort, while others have more intense pain.

Bloating

Hormonal changes can contribute to fluid retention and bloating around menstruation.

Breast Tenderness

Hormonal fluctuations may cause breast swelling, sensitivity or discomfort during parts of the cycle.

Mood and Energy Changes

Some people experience changes in mood, sleep, concentration or energy levels around their period.

Changes in Menstrual Flow

Menstrual flow can differ between individuals and from one cycle to another. Keeping track of significant changes may help during a healthcare consultation.

When Should You Seek Medical Attention?

Speak with a qualified healthcare professional if you experience:

  • Severe period pain that disrupts everyday activities.
  • Bleeding that lasts longer than seven days.
  • Repeated bleeding between periods.
  • Periods that suddenly become much heavier or different.
  • Persistent pelvic pain, even outside menstruation.
  • Menstrual symptoms that interfere with school, work or sleep.

When It May Be an Emergency

Seek urgent medical care for very heavy bleeding accompanied by fainting, severe dizziness, weakness or difficulty breathing; sudden severe pelvic or abdominal pain; or severe pain or bleeding when pregnancy is possible.

If you feel seriously unwell, do not wait for an online explanation.

Record What You Experience

Writing down when symptoms occur, how severe they feel and how they affect your daily activities may help you explain them during a healthcare consultation.

Do Not Self-Diagnose

Symptoms can have different causes. Conditions such as endometriosis, PCOS, fibroids and other health problems require appropriate professional assessment.

MATONI provides educational information. It does not diagnose conditions or prescribe treatment.
