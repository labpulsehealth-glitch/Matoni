PERIOD WITHOUT SHAME

Every girl deserves access to period products.

You should never have to miss school, feel embarrassed, or manage your period without the products you need. If you need menstrual products, we want to help connect you with support.

OUR COMMITMENT

Support without shame.

We envision partnerships with schools, NGOs, community organisations, product manufacturers and donors to help girls access menstrual products discreetly and safely.

No girl should be mocked, excluded or made to feel inferior because she cannot afford sanitary products.
