PERIOD WITHOUT SHAME

Every girl deserves access to menstrual products.

Menstrual dignity should never depend on how much money a girl or her family has.

Menstrual Support and Access

MATONI aims to help girls who have difficulty accessing menstrual products by connecting them with verified support organisations and community partners.

A request could be made for yourself or, where appropriate, on behalf of someone you are helping, such as a friend, sibling or student.

What Products Could Be Supported?

  • Disposable sanitary pads.
  • Reusable menstrual pads.
  • Menstrual underwear.
  • Menstrual hygiene kits.

Availability will depend on participating partners and local needs.

How the Programme Is Intended to Work

  1. A girl or authorised helper submits a confidential request.
  2. A verified support partner reviews the request.
  3. Available products and collection arrangements are confirmed.
  4. Products are provided through an appropriate, discreet channel.

This is the proposed service model. MATONI has not yet activated a live distribution network.

Request Demonstration

This is a prototype demonstration of the proposed request process. Do not enter your real name, address, phone number or other personal information.

Your Privacy Matters

A future live service must protect the identity and dignity of people requesting support. Requests must not be publicly displayed, and intimate photographs or unnecessary sensitive information must never be required.

Any service involving minors will require appropriate safeguarding, privacy and consent arrangements.
