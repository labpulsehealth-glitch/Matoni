SAFETY • PRIVACY • SUPPORT

Your safety and dignity matter.

HERGUARD is our vision for a safer, more supportive environment where women and girls can find information about protection, trusted support and available resources.

What we want to build

Personal safety should not be treated as an afterthought in women's health and wellbeing.

Trusted support resources

Help users discover appropriate support organisations and services.

Privacy and confidentiality

Design experiences that protect personal information and minimise unnecessary data collection.

Safety education

Provide age-appropriate information about boundaries, consent, personal safety and seeking help.

Support pathways

Help users understand how to approach trusted adults, qualified professionals and appropriate support services.

Our responsibility

HERGUARD is currently a planned division, not an emergency response service or a live crisis-reporting system. We will only introduce safety tools after appropriate safeguarding, privacy and professional review.
