HOW WE SELECT RESOURCES

Credibility matters.

MATONI prioritises resources from recognised public-health agencies, government health services and established medical information organisations. A listing does not mean MATONI independently endorses every recommendation on an external website.

  • We identify the organisation responsible for each resource.
  • We link readers directly to the original publication or information page.
  • We distinguish external medical guidance from MATONI educational content.
  • We encourage readers to consider their local healthcare context.

Directory status: Initial curated collection. Links and source details should be checked periodically. MATONI has not independently clinically reviewed every external page.

Have a health concern?

Online information can help you understand a topic, but it cannot diagnose a condition. Speak with a qualified healthcare professional about symptoms, test results or treatment decisions.
