HERVOICE · COMMUNITY STANDARDS

Our community guidelines

Every girl and woman deserves to be heard without humiliation, intimidation or discrimination.

1. Respect every experience

Do not mock, shame, insult or dismiss someone because of menstruation, body changes, health conditions or personal experiences.

2. No bullying or harassment

Threats, intimidation, sexual harassment, hate speech and targeted humiliation are not acceptable.

3. Protect people's identities

Do not publish another person's name, photograph, phone number, school, address, private messages or medical information without permission.

4. Be responsible with health information

You may share personal experiences, but do not present your experience as a medical diagnosis or tell another person to stop prescribed treatment.

5. Support, don't pressure

Listen without forcing someone to reveal more than they want. Avoid blaming people for their symptoms, circumstances or decisions.

6. Respect different experiences

People experience menstruation, reproductive health and body changes differently. Do not assume that everyone's body or circumstances are the same.

7. Reporting harmful content

Members should be able to report harmful stories and comments. The current HERVOICE report button is only a demonstration and does not send reports to a moderation team.

8. Our commitment

MATONI intends to develop a properly moderated community with clear reporting procedures, safeguarding measures and consequences for abusive behaviour before public community launch.
