MATONI · TRUST AND TRANSPARENCY

Your privacy matters.

Women's health information is personal. We believe every girl and woman deserves to understand how her information is handled.

1. Current website status

MATONI is currently an early-stage website prototype. It does not yet have user accounts, a central user database or a functioning online community backend.

2. HERVOICE stories and comments

In the current demonstration, stories and comments are held temporarily in the browser's active page session. They are not uploaded to a shared MATONI database. They may disappear when the page is refreshed or closed.

3. Cycle and health information

The current cycle estimate tool calculates an estimate in your browser. It is not a medical diagnosis or a confirmed prediction. Do not use the prototype as your only record of important health information.

4. Information you should not share

  • Your full name or home address.
  • Your phone number or personal identification numbers.
  • Your school, exact location or other details that identify you.
  • Private medical documents or intimate photographs.

5. Website hosting

MATONI is hosted through GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical information such as access logs under its own policies. The absence of a MATONI database does not mean that website hosting involves no technical data processing.

6. Future development

Before introducing accounts, persistent stories, health records or other data services, MATONI intends to establish appropriate privacy notices, security measures, consent processes and data protection controls.

7. Important notice

This page describes the current prototype. It is not a substitute for a complete legal privacy policy. The policy will need to be reviewed and updated before the platform launches services that collect personal information.
