Support · Safety · Protection

Get Help Now

You deserve to feel safe, respected and supported. Whatever you are experiencing, you deserve to be taken seriously. You do not have to face everything alone.

Are you in immediate danger?

If you are in immediate danger, try to move to a safer place and contact emergency services or someone you trust who can help you right now. Do not wait for an online report to receive help.

Your voice matters

Report to MATONI

If you are experiencing abuse, threats, coercion, exploitation or a situation that makes you feel unsafe, you can report it here. You can choose to remain anonymous.

Share only what you feel comfortable sharing. You do not need to describe everything.
Your safety and privacy matter. Please avoid including passwords, bank details or information that could unnecessarily put you at risk. Your report should only be accessible to authorised MATONI personnel.

If you are worried about your safety

You know your situation best. Choose the steps that are safest for you. Seeking help does not mean you have to do everything at once.

  • Move to a safer place if you can do so without increasing danger.
  • Reach out to someone you trust who will take you seriously.
  • Seek medical care if you are injured or need urgent medical attention.
  • Avoid confronting someone if doing so could put you in danger.
  • Be careful if someone monitors your phone, messages or internet activity.
