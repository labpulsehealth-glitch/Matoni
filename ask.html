Why do periods happen?

During the menstrual cycle, hormones help prepare the uterine lining for a possible pregnancy. When pregnancy does not occur, hormone levels change and the lining is shed through menstruation.

Why can periods be irregular?

Cycle changes can occur during adolescence, with stress, changes in weight, illness, intense exercise and hormonal conditions, among other reasons.

Persistent irregularity or other concerning symptoms should be discussed with a healthcare professional.

Understanding PCOS

Polycystic ovary syndrome is a hormonal and metabolic condition that can affect menstrual cycles, ovulation and other aspects of health.

Having irregular periods or acne alone does not establish a diagnosis. A healthcare professional must assess the individual and consider other possible causes.

Understanding Endometriosis

Endometriosis is a condition in which tissue similar to the lining of the uterus grows outside the uterus. It may cause pelvic pain, painful periods and sometimes fertility difficulties.

Severe menstrual pain should not automatically be dismissed as normal.

Understanding Fibroids

Uterine fibroids are growths that develop in or around the uterus. They are usually non-cancerous.

Some people have no symptoms, while others may experience heavy menstrual bleeding, pelvic pressure or pain.

Understanding Hormones

Hormones are chemical messengers that help regulate many body functions. Oestrogen, progesterone, follicle-stimulating hormone and luteinising hormone all play roles in reproductive health and the menstrual cycle.
