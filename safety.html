MATONI · HEALTH AND SAFETY

Your health deserves attention.

MATONI exists to make women's health information easier to understand. It does not replace professional medical assessment or emergency care.

1. Information, not diagnosis

Educational articles, cycle estimates and symptom information are intended to support understanding. They cannot confirm whether someone has a medical condition.

2. When to seek medical attention

Speak with a qualified healthcare professional if you experience:

  • Period pain that regularly interferes with school, work or daily activities.
  • Very heavy or prolonged menstrual bleeding.
  • Repeated dizziness, weakness or unusual fatigue.
  • Persistent pelvic pain or unusual changes in your cycle.
  • Symptoms that are new, worsening or worrying you.

3. When it may be urgent

Seek urgent medical care if you faint, experience severe or sudden pelvic or abdominal pain, have very heavy bleeding accompanied by weakness or dizziness, or have difficulty breathing or chest pain.

If someone is unconscious or seriously unwell, contact local emergency services or get them to an appropriate emergency facility. Do not wait for an online response.

4. Medical information in the community

HERVOICE is designed for sharing experiences and peer support. Comments from other members are not medical advice. Do not delay seeking care because of something someone says online.

5. Young users and safeguarding

MATONI recognises that some users may be minors. Future community services must have age-appropriate safeguards, suitable consent processes, privacy protections and procedures for responding to disclosures of abuse or immediate danger.

6. No emergency response service

HERGUARD is a planned safety initiative. It is not currently an emergency hotline, crisis service or live protection service.

7. Our responsibility

We aim to use reliable health information, communicate uncertainty honestly and avoid making promises about medical outcomes or services that are not yet available.
