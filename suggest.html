Research Suggestion Box

What should science investigate next? Tell us about a research question, a neglected health concern, or an area where more evidence is needed.

Please read before you send:
  • This box is for research ideas, not personal medical problems. For health questions, try Ask MATONI or visit a clinic.
  • If you or someone else is in danger or needs urgent help, please use Get Help Now.
  • You can stay anonymous. If you are under 18, please leave the name blank.
  • Please do not include phone numbers, email addresses, or details that could identify you or someone else.
  • Suggestions are read by the MATONI team and are not published. We cannot reply to each one.
